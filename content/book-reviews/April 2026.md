# Blood of Hercules (Villains of Lore, #1)
**By Jasmine Mas**

![[../images/Blood of Hercules.jpg|200]]

> "I was what they’d made me into. And they hated it." *- Jasmine Mas, Blood of Hercules*

### Book Review
*Blood of Hercules* by Jasmine Mas is book one of the author's Villains of Lore series. This book was definitely not what I imagined it to be. It was spicy. It had adult humor and activity that was not for everyone but I truly enjoyed reading it and could not wait to start book two. The characters had extreme emotions and reactions and I can warn anyone considering this book that it does have gory scenes and intimate sessions that may be over whelming or too much for some. The main character had a lot of drowsy emotions in the book that I tended to feel like I was that way as well. So read with caution but keep an open-mind.
