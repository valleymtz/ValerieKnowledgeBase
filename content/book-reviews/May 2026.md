> ""I'm yours," I said as the rain roared around us. "You can break my legs, but I'll still crawl after you. If you're injured, I will carve myself to pieces to make you whole... every... single... time. This is not a temporary arrangement. We will be together. Forever. Or I'll be dead."" *- Jasmine Mas, Blood of Hercules*

### Book Review
*Bonds of Hercules* by Jasmine Mas is book two in the author's Villains of Lore series. The love story we craved in book one is finally here. Book two still had the humor we loved in book one and add character evolution so much that we began to love some of the characters we hated in book one. The best advice I can give is if you were iffy about book one just keep reading and give it a chance.
